Title: TrianglesNo2 - Watch triangles dance to the beat!

Description:
Ever seen geometry groove? TrianglesNo2 turns sound into living, breathing art where every kick and snare makes triangles explode in the corners while the main fractal pulses with the melody.

It's like watching math have a dance party - color schemes shift, backgrounds wash in and out, and everything's perfectly synced to original audio made in Reason Studios. Built with p5.js, WebGL, and Tone.js for that sweet spot between code and feeling.

Key elements:
- Main Sierpinski fractal that evolves with the melody (think: musical evolution)
- Drum-reactive mini-triangles in corners - kick hits bottom-left, snare hits bottom-right
- Color schemes that reset on specific musical cues for visual surprise
- Full-screen background pulses that breathe with sustained notes
- All driven by real-time audio analysis and MIDI triggering

Perfect for anyone who loves seeing music visualized in unexpected ways.

Built with: p5.js, WebGL, Tone.js
Audio: Original composition in Reason Studios
Technique: Audio-reactive generative art that feels alive
